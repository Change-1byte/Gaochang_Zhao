Hi again! ☺

I'm Kate Zhao, a Computer Science master's student at NYU Tandon, focusing on Artificial Intelligence, with a CS background from UC Irvine and a love for games and human-centered experiences.

I like asking why, testing assumptions, and turning strange little ideas into real working projects. Outside of coding, I serve as Head of Events at NYU's Chinese Students and Scholars Association (CSSA), where I love bringing people together and turning ideas into memorable events.

When I'm not thinking about models or debugging code, I'm probably exploring a game, noticing an interesting interaction, or finding inspiration in everyday life.

My favorite kind of work? The kind where curious people build something unexpected together.
